## Problem

Epic `rtt` (id 13) is built by a Linux-only autonomous agent, but some of its work cannot be verified
from there: manual and hardware steps (demo screenshot, gaming measurement, 3-person call, Windows
and macOS runs), CI workflow edits that only a GitHub runner proves, and the upstream steps
(issue, export, PR, force-push) that `.claude/CLAUDE.md` keeps away from automation. Left inside the
buildable specs, they would block every close-out. This epic gathers them as specs to decide with
the human.

## Proposal

- Spec 8: demo screenshot, upstream feature-request issue and maintainer answer (from
  `rtt-transcript-panel` 3.2, epic 1.1, 1.2).
- Spec 9: STT latency/CPU measurement while gaming and the default model (from `rtt-stt-engine` 3.2).
- Spec 10: 3-person attribution test, threshold and event-offset tuning (from
  `rtt-speaker-attribution` 2.2).
- Spec 11: CI jobs for `--features rtt` (from `rtt-audio-capture` 2.2 CI half,
  `rtt-capture-windows-macos` 3.1).
- Spec 12: validation matrix, `feat/rtt` export and upstream PR (from epic section 3, plus the
  manual Validation bullets of epic 13 and spec 6).

## Out of Scope

- Buildable work; that stays in epic `rtt` (specs 2 to 6).
- Approving or executing anything on the human's behalf.

## Impact

### Product code this spec expects to touch

- `crates/orbolay-core/src/config.rs`, `crates/orbolay-rtt/src/attribution.rs`: tuned defaults
  (specs 9, 10)
- `.github/workflows/check.yml`, `.github/workflows/build.yml`: `--features rtt` CI (spec 11)

## Validation

- Every member (specs 8 to 12) is concluded by the human, with its evidence (URLs, measurements, run
  links) recorded in that member's ## Discoveries.

## Design

- A separate epic, because a store epic has one level and `--after` only takes labels of the same
  epic. Cross-epic order is therefore written in each member's ## Handoff: 8 after spec 2; 9 after
  spec 4; 10 after spec 5; 11 after spec 8's answer and specs 3 and 6; 12 after everything.
- Within this epic: 8 first; 9 and 11 after 8; 10 after 9 (both edit
  `crates/orbolay-core/src/config.rs`); 11 is file-disjoint from 9 and 10 (only
  `.github/workflows/`); 12 last.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keeping the tasks in the children and marking them blocked (`[!]`). Rejected: a blocked task keeps
  a buildable spec from closing, and the evidence these tasks need is not the executor's to produce.

## Open Decisions

- Whether epic `rtt` members 3 to 6 wait for spec 8's upstream answer (epic 1 Risks). The human
  decides; the store cannot express that edge.

## Risks

- The human steps lag the build, so merged code carries provisional defaults (`ggml-base`, 35%
  threshold, untuned offset). ACCEPTED: the defaults are configurable and documented as provisional
  until specs 9 and 10 conclude.

## Handoff

- Source plans: `specs/plans/rtt-epic.md` and its five children. Epic `rtt` is id 1 (members 2 to 6).
