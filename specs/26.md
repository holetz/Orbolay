## Problem

Reproduced by the epic 24 review (harness `target/quenching/review2/e2e`, public `MonoResampler` + `VadSegmenter`): 1 s silence, a jump to 5000 ms, speech at 5500 ms → `rate 48000 ch 2: speech really starts at 5500ms; final utt 1 start=1.24s end=3.57s`; VAD alone: `speech at ~5.64s; final start=520ms`. Spec 20 (PR #7) re-anchored the resampler's output timestamps, but `crates/orbolay-rtt/src/stt/vad.rs:103` only reads `frame.timestamp` when `pending` is empty and `vad.rs:112` otherwise advances by window count; at 48 kHz the resampler emits ~342 samples per frame against a 480-sample window, so a partial window is almost always pending. The wrong `segment.start` feeds attribution at `crates/orbolay-rtt/src/pipeline.rs:245`. Consequence: spec 20's harm persists in the real 48 kHz path — after a queue drop every loopback segment is stamped early by the dropped time (~4.3 s) and matched to the wrong Discord speaker. Test end to end (resampler → VAD), not the resampler alone.
