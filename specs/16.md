## Problem

The run of 2026-10-11 aligned only the specs front (`backend: git`, harness `.claude/CLAUDE.md`).
`/quenching:align` would also scaffold the `docs/` OKF bundle, `.claude/` commands and the
ops/proof/toolchain/delivery fronts. The upstream export in epic 13's `## Design` excludes only
`specs`, `.claude` and `.gitignore` (`git diff upstream/main develop -- . ':!specs' ':!.claude'
':!.gitignore'`), and `docs/` is already a tracked upstream folder (`docs/image/*.png`). Consequence:
any front that writes outside `.claude/` and `specs/` leaks into the PR to `SpikeHD/Orbolay`, so
choosing which fronts to align, and where they live, is a design choice for the human.
