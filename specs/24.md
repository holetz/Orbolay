## Problem

The epic 13 correctness review (2026-10-11, range 5c66a14..10b36fa on `develop`) reproduced five defects in the RTT pipeline: line IDs reused after a restart (18), provisional captions never finalised (19), resampler timestamps ignoring gaps (20), unchecked model names allowing path traversal (21), and a download without integrity check or timeout (22). Spec 17 adds the default build that pulls ALSA and whisper.cpp through the workspace member `orbolay-rtt`. Until these are fixed, the captions are wrong in ordinary use and the model download can write outside its folder.
## Design

Same branch model, gate and export rules as epic 13's `## Design` (base `develop`; `main` and `SpikeHD/Orbolay` never written; gate of `.claude/CLAUDE.md` including the `--features rtt` variants).

**Autonomy policy (2026-10-11, `/quenching:specs:autopilot` run, round 2).** Authorized by the human's autopilot invocation of 2026-10-11: after a verifier PASS, every member is executed, pushed to `origin` (holetz/Orbolay) and opened as a PR into `develop`. Merge certification, as decided by the human for epic 13: CI does not run on PRs into `develop`, so the conductor runs the full gate on the PR head and merges into `develop` when it is green; the orchestrator stops at the open PR.

Members come from the epic 13 correctness review (each `## Problem` carries the reproduction). A fix must ship a regression test that fails on the pre-fix code. Spec 17 must not edit CI workflows.
